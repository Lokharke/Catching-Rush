# Privacy Policy for Catching Rush

**Last updated: 09 October 2025**

This Privacy Policy describes how **Lokharke** ("we", "us", or "our") handles information in the mobile game **Catching Rush** (the "Game").

By installing or using the Game, you agree to the practices described in this policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the Game.

## Summary

Catching Rush is a single-player tap game that works entirely on your device. We do not require an account, we do not ask for your name or email, and we do not collect or store personal information on any server we control. The only third party that may receive data when you use the Game is Google, through the AdMob advertising service, for the purpose of showing ads and measuring their performance.

## Information We Do Not Collect

We do not collect:

- Your name, email address, phone number, or any contact information
- Your precise location
- Your photos, files, microphone, or camera
- Your contacts or calendar
- Any gameplay content or messages (the Game has no user-to-user communication)

The Game does not have user accounts, does not have a login, and does not send your gameplay data to us.

## Information Stored on Your Device

Catching Rush saves a small amount of data **locally on your device only**, using Android's SharedPreferences. This data never leaves your device and is not accessible to us. It includes:

- Your selected game mode (Simple, Color Match, Reverse, or Sequence)
- Your selected rod type (Rod, Bamboo, Wood, Bottle, Pencil, or Candle)
- Your best level and total catches for each game mode
- Your best score for each game mode
- A temporary save of an in-progress run, so the game can offer to resume it

You can clear this data at any time by uninstalling the Game or clearing the app's storage from your device settings.

## Information Collected by Advertising Partners

Catching Rush displays advertisements through **Google AdMob**, a service provided by Google LLC. When ads are requested or shown, Google may collect information from your device to serve ads, prevent fraud, and measure ad performance. This may include:

- Your device's advertising ID (a resettable, non-permanent identifier)
- Your IP address, which is used to estimate an approximate location (country or region level)
- General device information, such as the device model, operating system version, and language
- Interaction data with the ads shown (impressions, clicks, and whether a rewarded ad was completed)

We do not receive personal information from Google through this process. We only receive aggregate revenue reports.

For more information about how Google uses data from apps that show its ads, please see:

- [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)
- [How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites)
- [Google Ads Settings](https://adssettings.google.com) — to control ad personalization

## Rewarded Ads

Some ads in Catching Rush are **rewarded ads**, offered as an option when the player chooses to continue a run after a game over. You are never required to watch a rewarded ad; you can also choose to restart or return home. If you choose to watch a rewarded ad and complete it, you receive the reward (continuing your run) and AdMob's servers record the completion. If you close the ad early, the reward is not granted.

## Children's Privacy

Catching Rush is designed to be appropriate for general audiences and does not knowingly collect any personal information from children. We do not have user accounts, chat features, or any way for a user to submit personal data to us.

If you are a parent or guardian and have any concern about how the Game handles data, please contact us at the email address below and we will respond promptly.

If you are located in a jurisdiction that requires additional protections for children (such as the United States under COPPA, or the European Union under GDPR), and you believe your child has used the Game in a way that concerns you, please contact us.

## Data Retention

We do not store any personal data. The locally saved game data on your device remains until you uninstall the Game or clear its storage. Data collected by Google through AdMob is retained according to Google's own data retention policies.

## Your Rights and Choices

**Advertising ID.** You can reset or delete your advertising ID, and opt out of personalized ads, through your device settings:

- Android: **Settings → Privacy → Ads** (or **Settings → Google → Ads** on some devices)

**Delete local data.** Uninstall the Game, or clear its storage from **Settings → Apps → Catching Rush → Storage → Clear storage**.

**California residents.** We do not sell personal information. If you have questions about your CCPA rights, contact us.

**European Economic Area, United Kingdom, and Switzerland.** We do not process personal data ourselves. AdMob's processing is subject to Google's own legal basis and consent framework.

## Third-Party Services

The Game uses the following third-party service:

- **Google AdMob** — for displaying advertisements. Google's privacy practices are described at the links above.

We do not use analytics SDKs, crash reporting SDKs, or any other tracking services beyond AdMob.

## Data Security

Because we do not collect or store personal data, there is no personal data of yours for us to protect on a server. The Game communicates with Google's ad servers over HTTPS. The locally stored game data on your device is protected by Android's standard app sandbox.

## Changes to This Policy

If we change this Privacy Policy, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top and, if the change is significant, notify users through a note in the Game or in the Play Store listing. Continued use of the Game after a change means you accept the updated policy.
